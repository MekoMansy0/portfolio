About

I'm a Computer Engineering student at Northeastern University. The work I care about sits right where hardware meets software: designing and building something physical, then writing the firmware and code that brings it to life.

Most of what I know comes from building. I've flown an embedded sensor payload on a NASA high-altitude balloon, written firmware on microcontrollers, designed parts in CAD and 3D-printed them, and built software from scratch. I like taking a project the whole way, from a rough idea to something that actually works, and figuring out whatever I need to along the way.

PRJ-01HARDWARE / FIRMWARE / DATA ANALYSIS

NASA High-Altitude Balloon Payload

MaxIQ.Space · presented at Cal Poly, San Luis Obispo

A multi-board sensor payload I designed, wired, and tested to log atmospheric data over a full NASA balloon flight. I designed and 3D-printed the housing with chunked recording to survive power restarts, then analyzed the flight data in Python, tracing a CO2/UV anomaly to a volcanic sulfur-dioxide plume.
